Burned · account deletion

Delete your account_

Burned is published by Loic Tuchagues · Last updated 17 September 2026

The quickest way is inside the app, and it happens straight away. If you no longer have Burned installed, you can ask us by email instead.

In the app — immediate

  1. Open Burned.
  2. Tap the menu — the three lines in the top-left corner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Delete my account again to confirm.

Without the app — by email

Email contact@burn3d.com with the subject Delete my Burned account.

Include your friend code if you have one — it looks like KILN-4827 and is shown under your name in the app's menu. It is the only thing that lets us find your account, because Burned never asks for your email address, phone number or real name.

We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you never chose a name in Burned, it holds nothing that identifies you, and there is nothing we could find the account by. Uninstalling the app removes the only link to it.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its anonymous identifier
  • Your name and friend code
  • Your friends list — you also disappear from your friends' lists
  • Friend requests and invites you sent or received
  • Your block list
  • Your status and Nearby location

What is kept, and for how long

  • Round records and feedback — up to 24 months. These describe how a round went and are not linked to a person.
  • Reports made by you or about you — up to 24 months, so a report can still be dealt with after an account is deleted. Photos copied into a report are deleted after 90 days.

Game rooms, photos and anything typed during a round are already deleted when the round ends, so there is nothing left of those to remove.

Full details of what Burned collects are in the app privacy notice.
